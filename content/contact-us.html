Get In Touch

If you are an individual interested in helping with our tournament (in either volunteer or ES capacity), please join our email list here so that you may be included in any future MIT Science Olympiad sign-up forms.

If you are a coach or team interested in receiving emails from MIT Science Olympiad about registration deadlines, please join our email list here. NOTE: This is NOT the same as completing our Team Registration form, which is a separate form that will be sent out and must be completed every year you wish to compete at our invitational.

If you have any questions, comments, or concerns about Science Olympiad at MIT or about our invitational tournament, please feel free to email us at scioly@mit.edu. If you are a college student or science educator in the Boston area who would like to volunteer at our tournament, we’d love to hear from you!

For more information about Science Olympiad, visit www.soinc.org


For more information about MIT, visit web.mit.edu
