title: "2027 Registration Open"
date: 2026-09-16
---

Registration for the 2027 MIT Science Olympiad Invitational is now open and closes on **Friday, September 25 at 6:00 PM ET**. Please visit the [registration page](/registration/) during this window for relevant information and the registration form.

As a reminder, team acceptance is not determined on a first-come, first-served basis and will instead follow the [lottery guidelines](/registration/#registration-details).

The registration fee for this year's tournament is **$200**. Financial aid for this fee is readily available for any teams that need it, and applications will open after registration closes.

Please note that you must submit a registration form regardless of how your team placed at the MIT Invitational or Nationals last year.

Feel free to reach out to us at [scioly@mit.edu](mailto:scioly@mit.edu) with any questions.
